About BVG Academy

Built by a formulator who has run the business herself

BeeVerduraGlow Academy was founded on a simple premise: cosmetic science training in Nigeria should be rigorous, hands-on, and honest about what it takes to build a real brand — not just a certificate on a wall.

Every faculty is designed and taught by practicing formulators and wellness entrepreneurs who have built, priced, and sold their own products in the Nigerian market. You learn the chemistry and the commercial reality together.
